    EDUCATIONAL CRYPTANALYSIS NOTICE: MD5 IS CRYPTOGRAPHICALLY BROKEN

    Under RFC 6151, MD5 is completely deprecated for security certificates and digital signatures. Practical collision attacks (Wang et al., Flame malware) allow attackers to generate two distinct inputs yielding identical 128-bit MD5 hashes. Modern applications must use SHA-256 or SHA-3.
